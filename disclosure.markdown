# Disclosure

Some posts on this site include affiliate links to parts, tools, and books. If
you buy something through one of those links, I may earn a commission at no
additional cost to you.

**As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.**

## What that does and does not mean

- I only link things I have **actually used**. If a part appears in a parts
  list, it was on my bench.
- **Nothing here is sponsored.** No manufacturer pays for coverage, supplies
  review units in exchange for a write-up, or sees a post before it publishes.
  If that ever changes, the post in question will say so at the top, not down
  here.
- Affiliate links are marked `rel="sponsored nofollow"` in the page source, and
  every parts list carries the disclosure directly above it rather than only on
  this page.

## A bias you should know about

I write about things worth using. When something turns out to be bad, fake, or
simply not worth anyone's time, I generally don't write about it at all rather
than publish a takedown.

That is a deliberate editorial choice, and it has an obvious consequence: **this
site skews positive.** The absence of a part here is not evidence against it,
and the presence of one means it cleared my bar, not that it beat everything
else on the market.

Where a post does report that something did not work, read it narrowly. It
usually means *did not work for this specific job* --- a particular speed, a
particular signal, a particular board --- not that the part is bad. Those
results are measurements from my bench, not verdicts on a product.

## Prices and availability

Component prices move and listings disappear, especially for the small breakout
boards this site covers. A link that worked when a post was published may point
somewhere useless a year later. Part numbers are given in the text as well as
in the links, so you can search for the part yourself --- which is often the
better move anyway.

## No tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising network, and no third-party scripts.
Search runs entirely in your browser. Affiliate links are ordinary links: the
destination site knows you arrived from here, and I see nothing beyond whatever
aggregate reporting that program provides.

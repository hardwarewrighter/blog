The disclosure. US FTC guidance requires affiliate relationships to be disclosed clearly and near the links --- not buried in a footer or a separate page. Amazon's Associates agreement additionally requires specific wording. `site.affiliate_disclosure` carries it, so it cannot be forgotten on an individual post. 2. rel="sponsored nofollow". Google requires paid and affiliate links to be marked; unmarked affiliate links are a manual-action risk for the whole site. Set once here so no post has to remember it.
